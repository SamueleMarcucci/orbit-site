# Live Orbit Product Marketing Context

## Product

Live Orbit is a prelaunch native iPhone satellite tracker published by Apps Made Better LLC.

Primary search positioning:

Live Orbit | Native Satellite Tracker for iPhone

Avoid using "AI-powered satellite tracker" as the main title. AI can be mentioned as a product feature through Orbit Intelligence, but the top-level positioning should stay understandable and iPhone-native.

## Publisher

Apps Made Better LLC is the publisher. The company site is https://appsmadebetter.com/.

## Audience

- Space-curious iPhone users who want to know what satellites are overhead
- Satellite watchers who want visible pass timing and direction
- People following Starlink, launches, crew missions, and space news
- Beginners who need the app to explain what to look for instead of showing raw orbit tables

## Core Promise

Track satellites live from your iPhone.

## Strongest Product Differentiators

- Native iPhone-first product experience
- Live 3D Earth view with real satellite positions
- Visible pass planning with local viewing context
- Sky Mode pointing for finding satellites overhead
- Orbit Intelligence for launches, crew missions, news, reentries, close approaches, and source-backed context
- Space news, launches, space weather, and beyond-Earth objects in one app

## Current Availability

Live Orbit is prelaunch. The website may invite users to apply for TestFlight, but must not claim public App Store download availability until a real public listing exists.

## Claims To Avoid

- Fake App Store ratings or reviews
- Fake user counts
- Fake satellite counts
- Fake rankings, awards, or press quotes
- Public download claims before release
- Safety-critical use claims
- iOS version claims unless confirmed by the actual app target and launch copy

## Safety Copy

Live Orbit provides estimates for personal satellite awareness. It is not for navigation, collision avoidance, satellite control, emergency operations, or safety-critical decisions.
