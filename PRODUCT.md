# Live Orbit Website Product Context

Register: brand

Live Orbit is a prelaunch iPhone app for following satellites, visible passes, Sky Mode and AR pointing, search, insights, news, and trusted orbital data.

Primary users are satellite watchers, space-curious beginners, and orbital-data enthusiasts who need a site that feels precise, trustworthy, and launch-ready without pretending the app is already on the App Store.

The website must not use App Store badges, fake usage numbers, fake satellite counts, fake operators, fake timestamps, fake mission claims, or fake availability claims. The primary action is joining the launch list through `mailto:support@liveorbitapp.com?subject=Live Orbit launch list`.

Tone: mechanical, exacting, field-issued. Simple copy, confident claims, clear safety limits.

Anti-references: generic dark SaaS landing pages, purple AI gradients, rounded icon-card grids, fake dashboard telemetry, hero metric templates, app-store-download claims before launch.
